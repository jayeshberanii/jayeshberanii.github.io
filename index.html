01 / 05HR Tech · Product

Employee offboarding & benefits portal

A portal that walks a departing employee through everything that follows their last day — benefit elections, insurance plans, and coverage for multiple dependents, each with its own eligibility rules.

I built the responsive front end in React and MUI from Figma handoffs, and the Node.js and Express services behind it. The hard part was never the UI: it was making dependent coverage rules readable to someone reading them on the worst week of their job.

02 / 05Integrations · Backend

HRIS sync across four platforms

Employee data lived in whichever HRIS the client already used. I integrated Merge API, Finch, PrismHR and iSolved so records synced into our platform automatically instead of arriving as spreadsheets.

Each provider models an employee differently, so the work was a normalisation layer, retry and reconciliation handling, and enough logging to answer “why is this person's status wrong” without opening a database console.

03 / 05Infrastructure · AWS

Multi-AZ AWS migration & DNS cutover

Designed and deployed the production environment: VPC, Application Load Balancer, EC2 behind Auto Scaling groups across two availability zones, RDS and ElastiCache Redis — the topology shown at the top of this page.

Ran the application migration and the Cloudflare DNS cutover, then wired up CloudWatch alarms and log groups so failures surface before users report them. Availability held above 99.9% after the move.

04 / 05Payments · Growth

Subscription billing with Stripe

Built the subscription flow and Stripe integration for a business development website — plan selection, checkout, webhook handling for renewals and failures, and the account states that follow from each.

Shortening the path from “interested” to “paying” is mostly a state-machine problem. Every webhook Stripe can send has to land the account somewhere sensible, including the ones that arrive twice.
